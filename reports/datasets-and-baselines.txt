DATASETS AND BASELINES

Our Qwen3 run trained on 4,404 mixed rows: 2,202 human and 2,202 AI.
The mixed split contains 1,542 paper-abstract rows from PMC and ACL,
plus 2,862 general-text rows from EditLens. The other training splits
below were used for local baselines, not for a separate Qwen run.

WHAT "MIXED" MEANS

"Mixed" combines two types of text in one balanced human-versus-AI split:
about 35% scholarly paper abstracts from PMC Open Access and ACL Anthology,
and 65% general writing from EditLens (including reviews, news, educational
web text, and Reddit writing prompts). Each class has the same target mix.
The paper AI examples were generated locally with Qwen2.5-0.5B and
SmolLM2-1.7B; the EditLens AI examples include Claude, GPT, and Gemini.
The 4,404-row mixed training split has 771 human and 771 AI paper rows,
plus 1,431 human and 1,431 AI general-text rows.

TRAINING SPLITS

Mixed: 4,404 rows (Qwen3 and mixed baselines)
PMC + ACL papers: 1,542 rows (paper baselines)
EditLens general text: 37,568 available; baselines used 20,000 rows
PMC-only abstracts: 616 rows (PMC-only baselines)

All binary training splits are balanced human and AI.

BASELINES

1. Character 3-5-gram TF-IDF + logistic regression
   Mixed: 200, 1,000, 3,000, and 4,404 training rows
   Papers: 100, 400, 1,000, and 1,542 training rows
   EditLens: 20,000 training rows
   PMC-only: 616 training rows

2. Word 1-2-gram TF-IDF + logistic regression
   Mixed: 3,000 training rows
   Papers: 1,542 training rows
   EditLens: 20,000 training rows
   PMC-only: 616 training rows

3. MiniLM embeddings + logistic regression
   Same training-row counts as the word TF-IDF baseline.

4. EditLens RoBERTa-large
   Published pretrained checkpoint. Its upstream training count is
   separate from our splits.

5. EditLens Llama-3.2-3B
   Published pretrained checkpoint. Its upstream training count is
   separate from our splits.

6. Load-bearing PR vocabulary model
   Frozen external model; no training on our labels.

Qwen3 is our model under comparison. Its two plotted decision thresholds
use the same trained weights; they are not two training runs.
