DATASETS AND BASELINES

Our Qwen3 run trained on these 4,404 rows (2,202 human; 2,202 AI):

  PMC Open Access paper abstracts: 618 rows (309 human; 309 AI)
  ACL Anthology paper abstracts: 924 rows (462 human; 462 AI)
  EditLens general writing: 2,862 rows (1,431 human; 1,431 AI)

EditLens includes reviews, news, educational web text, and Reddit writing
prompts. The paper AI examples were generated locally with Qwen2.5-0.5B
and SmolLM2-1.7B; the EditLens AI examples include Claude, GPT, and
Gemini. The other training splits below were used for local baselines,
not for a separate Qwen run.

TRAINING SPLITS

PMC + ACL + EditLens combined: 4,404 rows (Qwen3 and combined baselines)
PMC + ACL papers: 1,542 rows (paper baselines)
EditLens general text: 37,568 available; baselines used 20,000 rows
PMC-only abstracts: 616 rows (PMC-only baselines)

All binary training splits are balanced human and AI.

BASELINES

1. Character 3-5-gram TF-IDF + logistic regression
   PMC + ACL + EditLens combined: 200, 1,000, 3,000, and 4,404 rows
   Papers: 100, 400, 1,000, and 1,542 training rows
   EditLens: 20,000 training rows
   PMC-only: 616 training rows

2. Word 1-2-gram TF-IDF + logistic regression
   PMC + ACL + EditLens combined: 3,000 training rows
   Papers: 1,542 training rows
   EditLens: 20,000 training rows
   PMC-only: 616 training rows

3. MiniLM embeddings + logistic regression
   Same training-row counts as the word TF-IDF baseline.

4. EditLens RoBERTa-large
   Published pretrained checkpoint. Its upstream training count is
   separate from our splits.

5. EditLens Llama-3.2-3B
   Published pretrained checkpoint. Its upstream training count is
   separate from our splits.

6. Load-bearing PR vocabulary model
   Frozen external model; no training on our labels.

Qwen3 is our model under comparison. Its two plotted decision thresholds
use the same trained weights; they are not two training runs.
